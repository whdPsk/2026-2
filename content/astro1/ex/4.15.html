\(r_e=e^2/m_ec^2\)와 \(\sigma_T=\tfrac{8\pi}{3}r_e^2\)를 계산하고, 전자 밀도 \(n_e=10^{26}\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}\)(태양 중심)와 \(10^3\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}\)(HII 영역)에서 광자의 평균자유경로 \(\ell=1/(n_e\sigma_T)\)를 구하라. [[L06.s7]]
풀이상수 대입
  1. \(r_e=(4.803\times10^{-10})^2/(9.109\times10^{-28}\cdot(2.998\times10^{10})^2)=2.82\times10^{-13}\) cm. [[S0917.18]]
  2. \(\sigma_T=\tfrac{8\pi}{3}(2.82\times10^{-13})^2=6.65\times10^{-25}\,\mathrm{cm^2}\) — PPT "0.665×10⁻²⁴ cm²"와 일치. [[S0917.19]]
  3. \(\ell=1/(10^{26}\cdot6.65\times10^{-25})=0.015\) cm; \(\ell=1/(10^3\cdot6.65\times10^{-25})=1.5\times10^{21}\) cm ≈ 0.5 kpc.
