PPT Q2 · 9/8 수업 문제
Assuming that the depth of the cloud (with τ_ν = 0.274) along the line of sight is 5 pc, find the number of absorptions per cm, κ_ν, in the cloud at the frequency of the line. Assuming that the cloud density is constant, how deep in pc would the line of sight distance have to be for the cloud to become optically thick?
(번역) τ=0.274, 시선 깊이 5 pc일 때 κ_ν는? 밀도 일정하면 몇 pc여야 광학적으로 두꺼워지나(τ=1)? [[L03.s10a]]
풀이
  1. 균일: \(\tau=\kappa L\), L = 5×3.086×10¹⁸ = 1.543×10¹⁹ cm. [[S0908.31]]
  2. \(\kappa=0.274/1.543\times10^{19}=1.78\times10^{-20}\) cm⁻¹.
  3. \(l=1/\kappa=5\,{\rm pc}/0.274=18.2\) pc.
결과

κ_ν = 1.78×10⁻²⁰ cm⁻¹, l = 18.2 pc — PPT 답과 일치. [[L03.s10b]]

+α 같은 구름이 T=83 K일 때 κ=2.6×10⁻¹⁵n_Hφ/T로 n_H를 추정하려면 무엇이 더 필요한가?

Answer

선중심 φ(ν₀)=1/(√πΔν_D), 즉 선폭. 예: FWHM 10 km/s면 Δν_D=6.0 km/s/λ=28.5 kHz → φ(ν₀)=1.98×10⁻⁵ Hz⁻¹ → n_H=κT/(2.6×10⁻¹⁵φ)≈29 cm⁻³.
