풀이

(a) \(t_r=t-r/c\), \(\mathbf p(t_r)=\tfrac12\ddot p_0t_r^2\hat{\mathbf z}\), \(\dot{\mathbf p}=\ddot p_0t_r\hat{\mathbf z}\), \(\ddot{\mathbf p}=\ddot p_0\hat{\mathbf z}\)(상수!). 편리한 조합:

\[ \mathbf p+\frac rc\dot{\mathbf p}=\ddot p_0\Big[\tfrac12t_r^2+\tfrac rct_r\Big]\hat{\mathbf z}=\tfrac12\ddot p_0\big(t^2-r^2/c^2\big)\hat{\mathbf z},\qquad \dot{\mathbf p}+\frac rc\ddot{\mathbf p}=\ddot p_0\,t\,\hat{\mathbf z}. \]

Prob. 10.35(Eq. 10.79)에 넣으면 (검산: \(V=\frac{\mu_0\ddot p_0}{8\pi}\cos\theta[(ct/r)^2-1]\), \(\mathbf A=\frac{\mu_0\ddot p_0}{4\pi c}[(ct/r)-1]\hat{\mathbf z}\) ✓)

\[ \boxed{\;E_r=\frac{\mu_0\ddot p_0}{4\pi}\frac{(c^2t^2-r^2)\cos\theta}{r^3},\qquad E_\theta=\frac{\mu_0\ddot p_0}{8\pi}\frac{(r^2+c^2t^2)\sin\theta}{r^3},\qquad B_\varphi=\frac{\mu_0\ddot p_0\,t\sin\theta}{4\pi r^2}\;} \]

(b) 지름방향 유동은 \(E_\theta B_\varphi\)에서만 온다(\(\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}\times\hat{\boldsymbol\varphi}=\hat{\mathbf r}\)):

\[ S_r=\frac{E_\theta B_\varphi}{\mu_0}=\frac{\mu_0\ddot p_0^2\,t\,(r^2+c^2t^2)\sin^2\theta}{32\pi^2r^5}. \] \[ P(r,t)=\oint S_r\,r^2d\Omega=\frac{\mu_0\ddot p_0^2t(r^2+c^2t^2)}{32\pi^2r^3}\cdot\underbrace{\frac{8\pi}{3}}_{\int\sin^2\theta d\Omega} =\boxed{\;\frac{\ddot p_0^2}{12\pi\epsilon_0r^3}\,t\Big[t^2+\Big(\frac rc\Big)^2\Big]\;} \]

(\(\mu_0(r^2+c^2t^2)=\frac1{\epsilon_0}(t^2+r^2/c^2)\) 사용.)

(c) \(P_{\rm rad}(t_0)=\lim_{r\to\infty}P(r,t_0+r/c)\). \(t=t_0+r/c\)를 넣으면

\[ t\Big[t^2+\frac{r^2}{c^2}\Big]=\Big(t_0+\frac rc\Big)\Big[t_0^2+\frac{2t_0r}{c}+\frac{2r^2}{c^2}\Big]\;\xrightarrow{\;r\to\infty\;}\;\frac rc\cdot\frac{2r^2}{c^2}=\frac{2r^3}{c^3}. \] \[ P_{\rm rad}=\frac{\ddot p_0^2}{12\pi\epsilon_0r^3}\cdot\frac{2r^3}{c^3}=\frac{\ddot p_0^2}{6\pi\epsilon_0c^3}=\boxed{\;\frac{\mu_0\ddot p_0^2}{6\pi c}\;}\;\checkmark\ \text{(Eq. 11.60)} \]

주의: \(\mathbf B\)가 \(1/r^2\)이라 "복사가 없다"고 속기 쉽지만, 필요한 것은 \(\mathbf B(r,t)\)가 아니라 \(\mathbf B(r,t_0+r/c)\)이고 여기엔 \(1/r\) 항이 들어있다.

감점 포인트

\(P(r,t)\)에서 \(t\)를 고정한 채 \(r\to\infty\)를 취해 "출력이 0"이라 결론짓는 것. 반드시 \(t=t_0+r/c\)로 파면을 따라가야 한다.
