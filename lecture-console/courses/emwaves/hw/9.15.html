풀이
\(\hat n_T=\cos\theta_T\hat x+\sin\theta_T\hat y\), \(\hat n_R=\cos\theta_R\hat x+\sin\theta_R\hat y\)로 두자. \(\hat k_I=\hat k_T=\hat z\), \(\hat k_R=-\hat z\), \(\tilde{\mathbf B}=\frac1v(\hat k\times\tilde{\mathbf E})\). \(\hat z\times\hat n=\cos\theta\,\hat y-\sin\theta\,\hat x\).
(iii) \(\mathbf E^{\parallel}\) 연속 (\(z=0\)): \[\tilde E_{0I}\hat x+\tilde E_{0R}\hat n_R=\tilde E_{0T}\hat n_T\ \Rightarrow\ \begin{cases}x:\ \tilde E_{0I}+\tilde E_{0R}\cos\theta_R=\tilde E_{0T}\cos\theta_T\\ y:\ \tilde E_{0R}\sin\theta_R=\tilde E_{0T}\sin\theta_T\end{cases}\] (iv) \(\frac1\mu\mathbf B^{\parallel}\) 연속: \[\frac{1}{\mu_1v_1}\Big[\tilde E_{0I}\hat y-\tilde E_{0R}(\cos\theta_R\hat y-\sin\theta_R\hat x)\Big]=\frac{1}{\mu_2v_2}\tilde E_{0T}(\cos\theta_T\hat y-\sin\theta_T\hat x)\] \(x\)성분: \(\dfrac{1}{\mu_1v_1}\tilde E_{0R}\sin\theta_R=-\dfrac{1}{\mu_2v_2}\tilde E_{0T}\sin\theta_T\).
여기에 (iii)의 \(y\)식 \(\tilde E_{0R}\sin\theta_R=\tilde E_{0T}\sin\theta_T\)를 대입하면 \[\tilde E_{0T}\sin\theta_T\left(\frac{1}{\mu_1v_1}+\frac{1}{\mu_2v_2}\right)=0 .\] 괄호는 양수이므로 \(\tilde E_{0T}\sin\theta_T=0\) → \(\theta_T=0\), 그러면 \(\tilde E_{0R}\sin\theta_R=0\) → \(\theta_R=0\). \[\boxed{\theta_T=\theta_R=0\ \text{— 편광은 반드시 입사파와 같은 방향}}\]
감점 포인트
\(y\)성분(전기장)과 \(x\)성분(자기장) 두 식을 모두 써야 한다. 하나만으로는 \(\theta_R,\theta_T\)가 결정되지 않는다.
